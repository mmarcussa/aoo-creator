Publish a fic collection

  1. Set up the collection.The gear beside Collection names your pack. Its namespace is permanent once you publish, so choose it deliberately.
  2. Add your authors.Every pseud gets a hidden permanent ID. Rename an author later and nothing breaks.
  3. Write.The Write tab is your manuscript. Add chapters down the left side; word counts keep themselves up to date.
  4. Fill in Details.Rating, archive warnings, tags and summary — the metadata readers browse on the Shard Market.
  5. Check Preview and Validation.Preview renders the work card the way the game will. Errors block the build; warnings are only advice.
  6. Save your project, then build the ZIP.Save project writes a .aoopack.json file. Build makes the mod you upload to Nexus.

Keep that .aoopack.json somewhere safe. Re-import it whenever you publish an update. Rebuilding a collection from scratch generates new internal IDs, and readers lose everything they recovered from the old version.
